Animal Walk

Move across the room like an animal, and back. Four animals, then a mix. About six minutes.

Stand the phone up sideways first, at about hip height, facing the room.

BearFace down on hands and feet. Crawl.
CrabFace up, hands behind you, hips up. Walk.
ChimpSquat low, hands up. Shuffle sideways.
FrogSquat, hop forward, land soft.

Before you tap

Stand the phone up sideways at about hip height — on a table, a stool, or a chair with a box on it — straight up, not tilted, with the screen facing into the room. Not on the floor: from down there it cannot see you standing. You need clear floor in front of it, about three steps deep and as wide as you can manage.

You only touch the phone once. After that it talks you into place and starts by itself. You never need to look at the screen: clear music means you're doing it right, muffled music means fix your shape, and a knock means turn around.

Done

If something felt wrong — it didn't see you, counted wrong, or muffled the music when you were doing it right — save the recording and send it. It holds exactly what the camera saw.
