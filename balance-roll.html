Balance: Roll

A ball sits on you and your hips roll it: down your body and back. Each trip there and back is one roll. About four minutes.

Put the phone on its side first, on the floor, facing the room.

BridgeOn your back, knees bent. Lower your hips and lift them.
CrabFace up on hands and feet. Dip your hips and lift them.
PlankFace down on hands and toes. Hips up into a peak, and back.

Before you tap

Put the phone on its side (landscape), on the floor, leaning on something so it stays upright, with the screen facing into the room. Get down about two steps back, side-on to it, so it sees you from head to feet.

You only touch the phone once. After that it talks you into place and starts by itself. The screen shows you, the line the ball runs on, and the ball. You can also play by ear: the sound falls as the ball rolls away, a low knock means it reached the end, and a bright ding means it is home: one roll.

Done

If something felt wrong — it would not give you the ball, did not count a roll, or could not see you — save the recording and send it. It holds exactly what the camera saw.
