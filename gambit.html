Gambit is played sideways. A phone camera is physically landscape: held upright it hands over a picture wider than it is tall and most of the screen goes black. Turned sideways you stand in exactly the same place and fill the picture.

GAMBIT

Chess puzzles you solve with your head and play with your body.

  • Study the position. There is no clock — thinking is free.
  • Raise both hands to choose.
  • Reach and hold a ring to pick the piece, then the square.
  • Squat to play the move. Every move costs work, right or wrong.
