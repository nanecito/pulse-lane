Balance

Hold a position on the floor and keep a ball on you. The longer it stays, the more it is worth. About five minutes.

Put the phone on its side first, on the floor, facing the room.

BridgeOn your back, knees bent. Lift your hips.
CrabFace up on hands and feet. Stomach flat like a table.
PlankFace down on hands and toes. One straight line.
Last ballCrab again. One ball, no second chance.

Before you tap

Put the phone on its side (landscape), on the floor, leaning on something so it stays upright, with the screen facing into the room. Get down about two steps back, side-on to it, so it sees you from head to feet.

You only touch the phone once. After that it talks you into place and starts by itself. You never need to look at the screen: a soft tick means the ball is on. A rolling sound going lower means your hips are dropping. Going higher means they are too high. A thud means it fell.

Done

If something felt wrong — it would not give you the ball, dropped it when you were level, or could not see you — save the recording and send it. It holds exactly what the camera saw.
