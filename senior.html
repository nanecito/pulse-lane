Move with the camera

Three games you play sitting in a chair. Your phone's camera watches your arms; nothing to hold, nothing to wear.

SparksLights appear around you. Reach out and touch them.Reaching · about 5 minutes KeeperA ball comes toward the goal. Put your hand where it is going.Reaching · about 5 minutes Second LineA dancer shows you two arm moves. Copy them, then dance to the music.Dancing · about 4 minutes

Before you start

  1. Sit in a steady chair without wheels, with room to stretch your arms.
  2. Turn the phone on its side and stand it about two steps in front of you, at chest height.
  3. Tap a game, then tap Start and allow the camera. The phone talks you through the rest.
  4. Stop whenever you like. The Stop button is always there.

These are games for moving and having fun. They are not a medical test or treatment. If you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath, stop. If you have a health condition, check with your doctor before starting a new activity. The camera is read on your phone; no video is sent anywhere.
